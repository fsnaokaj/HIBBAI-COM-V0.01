Usage notes

The wordmark is always set in the bold italic outlined Hibbai! treatment — never recolor the outline, and never pair it with a different exclamation mark style. The blob mascot sits to the left of the wordmark, never above, below, or replacing a letter — coral-filled on white/dark backgrounds, white-with-ink-outline on coral backgrounds, so it never disappears into its background. On the shop, the wordmark carries the shop · chat · win tagline; sub-products (Forums, Shirt.Hibbai) drop the tagline for their own plain-text sub-label instead.
